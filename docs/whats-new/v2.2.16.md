# HopStat v2.2.16

Dark mode now follows one idea whatever your brand colour is: slate surfaces, with the brand only where something is selected or worth noticing.

## What's New

- **Brand-neutral dark theme** — the page, cards, sidebar and header use the same cool slate for every brand colour. The brand shows up as a translucent fill on the active menu item and icon tiles, and as the colour of buttons, badges, links and highlights. Green, navy, red or orange brands all read as the same product with a different accent.

## Fixes

- **Admin header alignment** — the logo block at the top of the sidebar and the content header are now the same height, so their bottom borders form one line.
- **Brand glow in dark admin** — the green glow, grid and sidebar shadow behind the admin pages are now neutral in dark mode, and panel borders no longer pick up the brand colour.
- **Removed BGP neighbors could come back** — a peer state event that arrived just after a neighbor was deleted re-registered it, so the neighbor still showed as live. Late events for removed neighbors are now ignored.

## Notes

- Internal notes on the hand-maintained store queries were corrected; nothing changed at runtime.
